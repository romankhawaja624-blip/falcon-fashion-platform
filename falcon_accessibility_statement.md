# FALCON Accessibility Statement

At FALCON, we are committed to ensuring that our AI-first fashion platform is accessible to everyone, regardless of their physical or cognitive abilities. We believe that premium fashion and cutting-edge technology should be inclusive by design.

## Our Accessibility Goals
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Every primary feature, from the AI Stylist to the Secure Checkout, is designed to be fully operable via keyboard.
- **Screen Reader Support**: We use semantic HTML and ARIA labels to ensure that our editorial content and AI-generated responses are clearly communicated to users of assistive technologies.
- **Contrast & Readability**: We maintain high-contrast ratios across our dark and light themes, ensuring that our typography remains legible under all conditions.
- **Reduced Motion**: We respect system-level 'Reduced Motion' preferences by simplifying transitions and disabling decorative background effects for users who find them distracting or physically uncomfortable.

## Interaction Methods
FALCON supports a variety of interaction methods, including touch, mouse, keyboard, and voice-assisted input. Our responsive layouts are optimized for screen sizes ranging from mobile smartphones to ultra-wide desktop monitors.
